My name is Tech, grew up in Thailand, Hong Kong and the United States where I've been living for the last 10+ years. Most of my professional life has been in Data and Systems engineering. I have dipped my toes into hardware engineering, materials science, commodity trading, e-commerce platform and marketing. Each of these experiences have been greatly rewarding and eye-opening. I also have a large range of personal interests that I think could be fun to share.

The way I look at life is like a large interactive book which I get to write. This book may not well be interconnected, but at the end of each chapter I hope to have learned something new or have a meaningful journey with people I met and treasure. When I was young, I used to quit often before things get good. One particular moment changed my perspective, and now I hate to quit without a clear purpose. So much so, sometimes it's like I'm not quitting enough. Clearly, there's still much to learn.

Roughly a quarter of my year, I spent writing codes or thinking about data and systems at work. A over a quarter, I spent sleeping (I probably average 7hr a-day.) Another fourth of a year is spent on sustenance like meals, cleaning, personal hygiene or taxes. The remaining is anything from personal projects, sports, social or anything else I explore. These temporal balance of my life typically get disrupted with being sick, getting stressed because of things I can't control, or some unfortunate delays in decision making. Each of which I try to minimize.
