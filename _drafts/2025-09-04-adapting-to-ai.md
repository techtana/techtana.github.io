Today, I am reminded again that many tasks that my team has to do will soon be done by AI. For example, looking up data available in the organization or building models off-the-shelf algorithm will be done by AI tools. Troubleshooting task can easily be removed as well by pairing a good prompt with enough context or fine tuning the LLM model with quality dataset. Data entry will also most certainly go away.

What surprises me is not the extent of tasks AI will do, but the speed at which it does. These automation taks have always been in our list of projects we plan to complete, and the AI-wave will accelerate the pace of completion. Either we build the solution to them quickly or other central AI team will hire people to do it quickly. We can no longer think of some tasks that require repetition to keep us in the job. So, it's logical to be the first mover to claim the value.

To be ahead of the transformation and not panic, it is important to understand what a Transformer-based AI like ChatGPT does. In short, it's a tool that predict the sequence of tokens; being words from books, answers to questions, codes from softwares, data sequence from the past, etc. 

For at least the next few years for data and programming jobs, we will still need human to verify accuracy of results for complex systems, evaluating metrics to answer business questions, exploring novel ideas or mixing multiple domains together, and setting up infrastructures and preparing systems for statistical machine to work effectively.

Many jobs in AI world will no longer require repetitive work, but something creative, require logical thinking, and making decisions.
