Roughly a quarter of my year, I spent writing codes or thinking about data and systems at work. A little more that a quarter, I spent sleeping (yes, I probably average 7hr a-day sleeping which is too little.) Another quarter of a year is spent on sustenance like meals, cleaning, personal hygiene or taxes. The final quarter is anything from personal projects, sports, social or anything else I explore. These temporal balance of my life typically get disrupted with being sick, getting stressed, or inability to make decisions. Each of which I try to minimize.

This space is where I share bits of my journey -- from things I pick up at work and hobibies I tinker with, to the sports and adventures that keep breaking me out of my mold. It's part journal of reflection, part reminder of lessons learned, and hopefully something intriging or relatable to those of you who found your way here.

I once heard Timothee Chalamet's award acceptance speech saying he is "in persuit of greatness". Not that he's a phillosopher or someone I particularly admire, but words at the right time struck a cord in me. What am I living to be? As cheesy or vulnerable as it sounds, I am still in a persuit of a life worth living. Rather than ruminating the impossible question of life, I'll speak it out to the cloud and hopefully get me to understand myself better in the process.
